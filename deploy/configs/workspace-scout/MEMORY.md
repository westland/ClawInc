## Research Quality Standards

### Citation Requirements
- Every finding must include source URL
- Publication date required for context
- Author/publication name for credibility assessment
- Quote key passages when relevant

### Relevance Scoring System
- **5/5**: Critical - Immediate action required, major industry shift
- **4/5**: High - Significant development, strategic implications
- **3/5**: Moderate - Worth noting, potential future impact
- **2/5**: Low - Background context, minor update
- **1/5**: Minimal - Tangential relevance, archived for completeness

### Verification Protocol
- Cross-reference major claims with 2+ independent sources
- Flag preliminary/unconfirmed information
- Distinguish between fact, analysis, and speculation
- Track source reliability over time
